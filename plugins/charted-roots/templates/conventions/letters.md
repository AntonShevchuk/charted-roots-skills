# Letters

`Letters/` holds outgoing requests to archives, registries and forums, plus `Letters Index.md`. **Most sensitive folder** — names living people, nothing redacted.

- No frontmatter; never type them `research_log_entry`.
- Filenames are unique snake_case slugs.
- Open with a `**Person:**` wikilink line, plus recipient, subject and status lines in the letter's language. Keep status current: Drafted / Sent / Answered — negative / Superseded / Refused.
- **`Letters Index.md`** is the register, grouped by research goal, one row per letter (person, target, status). Add a row per new letter; update on reply.
- **Written in the recipient's language — never translate, rephrase or tidy.** Labels stay in that language too. Summarise in English in the note that links to it.
- Filled forms (`.pdf`, `.docx`) sit beside the letter that sends them, linked from its body.
