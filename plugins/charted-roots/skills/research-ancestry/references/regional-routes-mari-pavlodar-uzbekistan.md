# Additional regional routes: Mari El, Pavlodar, Uzbekistan

A supplement to `seed-sources.md`, proposed by the author of [#140](https://github.com/DenisSergeevitch/ex-ussr-kinship-research/pull/140). Below, the author's observations are distinguished from the re-check of public pages on 02.11.2026; neither establishes the completeness of the fonds or access to each file. These sites are consulted only on the user's explicit instruction for the task (`SKILL.md` § Rules that win).

## State Archive of the Mari El Republic

At the re-check on 02.11.2026 the official [databases page](https://gosarhivrme.ru/ru/bazy-dannykh-gosudarstvennogo-arkhiva-respubliki-mariy-el) linked to [confession lists](https://churches.gosarhivrme.ru/), [revision lists](https://revisions.gosarhivrme.ru/) and [lists of people evacuated to the Mari ASSR in 1941–1945](https://evacuation.gosarhivrme.ru/). The navigation was checked, not the completeness of the indexes or the presence of any specific record.

In the sections on [state administration](https://gosarhivrme.ru/ru/gosudarstvennaya-vlast-i-gosudarstvennoe-upravlenie-0) and [agriculture](https://gosarhivrme.ru/ru/selskoe-khozyaystvo-0), also opened on 02.11.2026, tables of fonds with PDF inventories are published. According to the author, the inventories of selsoviets (village councils) and kolkhozes help find household books («похозяйственные книги») and payroll books («расчётные книги»). An inventory gives reference codes and dates, not the family composition and not access to the file's images; establish the type of book and the settlement from the full description. A kolkhoz name is not by itself the name of a village — do not create a Place note for a village from a kolkhoz name alone.

## Pavlodar: where to look for enterprise employees' records

The author cited «Межведомственный архивный справочник о местонахождении документов по личному составу предприятий, организаций, учреждений Павлодарской области: 1922–2010 годы» (Interdepartmental archival guide to the location of personnel records of enterprises, organisations and institutions of Pavlodar Oblast, 1922–2010), volume 1, and [a RAR address on the archive's site](https://www.arhiv.pavlodar.gov.kz/files/news/19/file_144_spravochnik_po_lichnomu_sostavu._tom_1.rar). According to his report of 02.11.2026, he read previously extracted text of a saved DOC; the original document was not re-rendered.

This guide is useful as a route from an enterprise to the fond and the named custodian of personnel records. The contents of the saved file have not been independently checked; at the repeat visit on 02.11.2026 the web tool also failed to open the RAR address and the site's home page. Current availability is unknown. Before a request, check the present custodian, inventories, periods and terms; a historical guide does not confirm that a specific person worked there or that a questionnaire exists. If the guide is obtained, it belongs in a `Reference -` note.

## Uzbekistan: database of evacuees

[evacuation.archive.uz](https://evacuation.archive.uz/) is a project of the «Узархив» (Uzarchive) agency: on the home page, re-opened on 02.11.2026, there is a search field by first name/surname and a statement of the agency.

According to the author's observation of 01.11.2026, the public search and cards opened without an account and gave list, page and number references. These cards have not been independently re-read. The composition and completeness of the database are not established; check a card's data against the original and resolve identity on the whole set of features. Absence of a name from the results checked does not exclude evacuation. The originals of the lists were not obtained in the author's observation.

## Uzbekistan: electronic catalogue and separate registrations

At the re-check on 02.11.2026 [e.archive.uz/login](https://e.archive.uz/login) returned a message that JavaScript is required. According to the author's observation of 01.11.2026, the standard catalogue search required authorisation; access to specific scans has not been established.

The information site has a separate [registration form archive.uz/user/register](https://archive.uz/user/register), open at the re-check on 02.11.2026. The existence of the form does not prove a shared account with e.archive.uz or access to scans. The user manages accounts themselves; the check of these pages did not include submitting forms or creating an account.
