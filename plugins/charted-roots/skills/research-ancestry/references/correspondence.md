# Requests to archives, libraries and other institutions

Read when drafting a family-history request or handling a reply. **The agent drafts only and never sends.** Every letter is a note in the letters folder, written in the recipient's language, and gets a row in the letters index (`.claude/conventions/letters.md`). The user sends it, through whatever channel the institution accepts, and tells the agent the outcome. A connected mailbox or browser does not change this.

## 1. Choose the recipient and the channel

Choose the current custodian of the record needed, by place, period and document type. First check its official website: whether it accepts such requests, which department answers, whether a specific form, a personal portal account or proof of kinship is needed. Record the URL and the date of the check in the letter note. Do not guess an e-mail address from the domain pattern. Note the intake channel: e-mail, a portal account, a state-services portal (госуслуги), a web form or paper mail. For e-mail, draft the letter text; for other channels, draft the text to be pasted into the form and list the form fields it fills.

| Whom to approach | What can be requested | What to give for the search |
|---|---|---|
| State, regional or municipal archive | Whether a metric-book entry, revision list (ревизская сказка) or family list (посемейный список) exists; a copy of a specific folio; where a fond is held | Historical place names, confession/parish if known, years, name variants, fond/opis/delo/list (фонд/опись/дело/лист) if known |
| ZAGS / DRATsS (ЗАГС / ДРАЦС, civil-registry office) or equivalent registration authority | The procedure for obtaining a birth, marriage or death record; a certificate or duplicate document where there is a right of access | Type and place of the event, date or range; check the required documents and access rights against the official procedure |
| Library, document-delivery service, newspaper department | A bibliographic reference, a permissible fragment of a book/dissertation abstract (автореферат), a search for a notice or obituary in a specific newspaper | Catalogue reference and shelfmark, author, title, year, pages; for a newspaper — place, period and kind of notice |
| Archive or library of a university, research institute or successor organisation | Where a personal/personnel file is held, orders on study or employment, thesis-defence materials, academic biography | The institution's name in the relevant period, years, faculty/department, thesis topic; do not assert an unknown position |
| Local-history museum, local library | Information on family collections, historical photographs, local press, deposited memoirs | Settlement, period, the person's occupation, event; ask for the reference and provenance of any material found |
| Cemetery administration or municipal burial service | Information from the historical burial register and how to obtain an extract | Full name, approximate date of death, cemetery name; do not request current contacts of living people responsible for the grave |
| Military or other departmental archive | Whether service, award, evacuation, repression or resettlement records exist and how to access them | Known dates, units/institutions, place of the event and file number; respect the user's bans on contacting particular agencies |

These are route options, not a promise that a record exists or will be released. Do not send the same request to every institution: for each recipient there must be grounds to regard it as the custodian or as a competent reference institution.

### Concrete examples of official entry points

Checked 26.10.2026; re-check contacts and terms before the user sends anything. The examples do not limit the geography of the research.

- **Российская государственная библиотека (Russian State Library):** [copying of fragments](https://store.rsl.ru/copy). You can ask whether a copy of the title page, its verso and the needed pages of a dissertation abstract can be made, by catalogue shelfmark. The page gives `mbadd@rsl.ru` for questions; orders go through the catalogue or a form. Ask about cost and permissible volume first if paid work has not been approved.
- **Российская национальная библиотека (National Library of Russia):** [electronic document delivery](https://store.nlr.ru/publish/servedd). You can request a permissible copy of an article or a fragment of a publication through this service, specifying the bibliography and terms. Do not promise a copy of an entire in-copyright book.
- **Bundesarchiv:** [research on persons and families](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/im-archiv-recherchieren/archivgut-recherchieren/personen-und-familienforschung/). For example, given a well-founded hypothesis of resettlement and naturalisation through the EWZ — find out whether the relevant material exists. Choose the department by file type; the page provides for a signed application sent by post or e-mail. The signature and any required personal documents come from the user.
- **Rigsarkivet (Denmark):** [ordering a search, scan or transcription](https://en.rigsarkivet.dk/services/purchase-an-archive-search-scan-or-transcription/). For example, the entry for a Russian WWI prisoner of war in the Danish Red Cross card index (`ww1-search.md`, section "Prisoners of war"). The page offers forms in English and German. As of 28.10.2026 — DKK 656 per hour excluding VAT; after the first hour, billing per half-hour. A reply within a month is stated for a scan or transcription, up to 10 months for other research; the digital answer and the invoice are sent separately. If another person orders on the applicant's behalf, a power of attorney is needed. The volume and the cap on billable hours are the user's decision; ordering and payment are the user's (section 3). Re-check terms before ordering.

For other former Soviet republics, find equivalent services on the official websites of local archives, libraries and successor institutions. Do not carry release rules or turnaround times over from one country to another.

**Requests from citizens and institutions of Belarus to the state archives of Ukraine.** The [2022 report of the State Archive of Luhansk Oblast](https://lg.archives.gov.ua/download/2023/annual_bulletin_2022.pdf) records a decision of Укрдержархів (the State Archival Service of Ukraine) to suspend correspondence for the period of martial law, and requests from citizens of Belarus being left without consideration on the merits. The [May 2022 report of the State Archive of Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast](https://if.archives.gov.ua/3035-2/) describes the suspension of processing of requests from Belarus. The procedure currently in force at a particular archive has not been established by this check. If the restriction may apply to the applicant, check the procedure against the recipient's official information before the user sends, and report the uncertainty. Give the applicant's details truthfully, to the extent the current form requires. Available opisi and published copies can be checked in parallel.

## 2. Channels the user will use

For state services and ZAGS records, the choice of service, applicant and delivery method — `government-portals.md`. Drafting and recording the letter follow sections 3–4.

1. Draft the text in the letters folder whatever the channel. For an e-mail channel, give recipient address, subject line and attachment list in the note so the user can send it as is. For a web form or portal, give the text and the field values ready to paste.
2. Login, signature, MFA and payment are the user's. Never ask for a password, MFA code, app password or OAuth token in the chat, and never store secrets in the vault. If the user wants to see the form, the agent may open the page in the browser (once web work is authorised) — login and CAPTCHA per `browser-and-captcha.md`, sections 5–5a — but it does not submit.
3. Carry on with other research checks while the letter waits on the user.

## 3. Draft the letter

- Formulate a narrow question and the result wanted. Give the known name variants, period, place, archival references and sources. Separate family story and hypothesis from what is documented. Do not invent, on the user's behalf, a degree of kinship, a representative's authority, an address or a signature.
- **Language of the request.** Choose it by the institution's requirements for the service and the submission channel. Check the language of the official form and the instructions for applicants. If the requirements do not specify a language, draft in the language of the suitable form or the institution's main working language. With several recipients, adapt the text to each.
- Check the constraints the user has stated for this task or recorded in the relevant `Search Plan -` note: permitted recipients/categories, sender, subject of the request, attachments and paid actions. A recipient the user has barred stays barred, including its sub-units; do not get around the ban through another channel.
- **Dedupe first.** Before drafting, read the letters index and search the letters folder for the same recipient and the same question: has this request been drafted or sent before, superseded, or answered? If so, extend or follow up on that letter instead of drafting a duplicate, and say so in the new letter.
- Attach only the documents needed, which the user agrees to give this recipient. Do not attach the whole family archive, passports, signatures or data on living relatives by default. Filled forms (`.pdf`, `.docx`) sit beside the letter in the letters folder and are linked from its body (`letters.md`).
- If paid work has not been approved, ask for terms and cost and include the sentence «Платное выполнение прошу начинать только после отдельного согласования» ("Please begin paid work only after separate agreement"), in the recipient's language. Never accept a contract, paid order or payment.
- **Payment from another country.** Before the user confirms a paid order, check the currency, the accepted payment methods and whether they are available to the user. Establish from the invoice or contract from what moment the deadline runs and the terms of any advance and refund. If no suitable payment method is available, leave the order pending the user's decision and continue other routes. Keep the invoice and the essential terms beside the letter in the letters folder.
- **Re-check just before sending.** Immediately before the user sends, re-read the latest notes relevant to the request, the letters index and any sent correspondence the user reports. If the same request has already gone, continue the existing thread instead.
- Keep the recipient list minimal — no unnecessary CC/BCC.

Example structure of a letter to a Russian-language archive (fill in with known data). The letter itself stays in the recipient's language: subject; one paragraph on the family being researched and what is already known from which source and what remains uncertain; the request — whether the specific record/file is held and whether a copy/certificate/reference can be obtained, with the known archival reference, and a request to be redirected if the documents went to another custodian; a request for the access procedure, documents, cost and timing, with the paid-work sentence; the applicant's name and reply address.

> Тема: Запрос записи о [событие, ФИО, период, место]
>
> Исследую историю семьи [ФИО и варианты]. По [источнику] известно [краткие сведения]; [неопределённость] пока требует проверки.
>
> Прошу сообщить, хранится ли у вас [конкретная запись/дело], и возможно ли получить [копию листа/справку/сведения о шифре]. Известный шифр: [если есть]. Если документы переданы другому хранителю, прошу подсказать, куда обратиться.
>
> Прошу уточнить порядок доступа, необходимые документы, стоимость и сроки. Платное выполнение прошу начинать только после отдельного согласования.
>
> [Имя заявителя и выбранный адрес для ответа]

For a recipient in another language, write the same structure in that language; do not translate a Russian draft mechanically.

### Choose the request type for the task

The patterns below are a basis for a draft, not a substitute for the institution's current form. Leave unknown details unknown; check the custodian's requirements on search period, documents, copies and cost. The quoted wording is for Russian-language recipients and stays in Russian.

- **Search for a record of an event.** «Прошу проверить запись о [рождении/браке/смерти] [имя и варианты] в [место, историческое и современное название] за [обоснованный диапазон лет]. Известны [родители/супруг, источник]. Предполагаемый приход и конфессия: [если известны]; для актовой записи — орган регистрации [если известен]. Прошу сообщить результат и, если доступно, предоставить справку и копию записи». (Asks the archive to check for a birth/marriage/death entry for the person in the place over a justified range of years, giving the known parents/spouse, presumed parish and confession or registry office, and asks for the result plus a certificate and copy.) Mention earlier requests and the preferred way of replying. Choose the range from the evidence and the archive's rules.
- **Children of a couple whose names are unknown.** Do not put an invented name in a mandatory field: the search may then be restricted to exactly that name. Give the known parents, place and a justified period and ask whether a search for their children's records is possible, and what scope, documents and cost that requires. If the form demands a child's name, ask the institution for the appropriate procedure; do not get around access restrictions. A negative answer for one name does not exclude other children.
- **Copy of a known file.** «Прошу сообщить, возможно ли получить копию [фонд, опись, дело, листы или нужный фрагмент], и уточнить стоимость и порядок заказа». (Asks whether a copy of the fond/opis/delo/folios can be obtained, and the cost and ordering procedure.) If it is unknown whether the file is digitised, ask separately about exporting existing scans and making new ones; do not assume they are one service.
- **Household composition from a household book (хозяйственная книга) or census.** «Прошу проверить [тип документа, место, период] на наличие хозяйства [имя домохозяина и варианты] и сообщить, возможна ли копия записи о составе семьи». (Asks for the document type, place and period to be checked for the household of the named head, and whether a copy of the family-composition entry is possible.) If the head of household is unknown, ask whether a surname search within a specific settlement is possible and on what terms.
- **Additional details from a civil-registration act record (актовая запись).** Where there is lawful access, ask the registration authority whether a certificate (справка) with the details of interest can be issued from the record of [event, person, place, period], and list the fields needed. Do not promise that the fields exist or will be released. Procedure, the applicant's rights and the delivery method — check per `government-portals.md`; if the record is missing from the electronic system, ask for the official procedure for finding and entering it. For Russia — the «Иные сведения» (other details) line in the certificate and checking against the paper record: `russia-records-access.md`, section 1.
- **Request to a Russian state or municipal archive.** Where the documents exist, archival certificates and copies for pensions, benefits and compensation are issued free of charge under [art. 26 part 3 of Law No. 125-ФЗ](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1406/be76d4e65d1676cb6d82f563ad9418f885157e36/). A search for family history may be done by the archive as a paid information service (part 4). For copies from repression files and filtration files covered by a special provision, see `russia-records-access.md`, section 2. State the purpose of the request, ask for a description of the volume checked and, if needed, ask about cost per section 3.
- **Departmental file.** For a military, medical, service or educational file, give the known years, institution or unit, identifying details and the catalogue reference; ask for the file's existence to be checked and the terms for a copy. Proof of kinship and authority go in under the general attachment rule above.

Before a paid order it is worth checking suitable catalogues, indexes and lawfully available copies, including local volunteer indexes, if that helps sharpen the request or avoid unnecessary cost. Choose a useful route per `source-strategy.md`; exhausting every online source before writing is not required (`government-portals.md`).

## 4. Record the letter and handle the reply

- The letter is a note in the letters folder (`<snake_case_slug>.md`): no frontmatter; a `**Person:**` wikilink line first, then recipient, subject and status lines **in the letter's language** (e.g. `**Кому:**` / `**Статус:**`, `**Status:**` in German). Below them: the official source of the contact (URL, date checked), the goal, the exact text to send and the list of attachments. Add a row to the letters index under the relevant research goal (person, target, status). Summarise the letter in English in the Search Plan or Hypothesis note that links to it.
- Status is what the user reports: Drafted / Sent / Answered — negative / Superseded / Refused. The agent never marks a letter Sent on its own inference; ask the user. Use only the `letters.md` statuses; if the user cannot confirm a letter went, leave it Drafted and say so in the note. Sent does not prove delivery or reading. Record a bounce, auto-reply, registration number (case/reference number) and a substantive reply separately, with dates, in the letter note.
- A reply and its attachments are artefacts with provenance and references. If a reply is evidence (it quotes or encloses a record), process it with `add-archive-record` — the reply or the enclosed copy becomes a Source; a letter paraphrasing a record and an enclosed copy of the same record are **one** archival item, never independent corroboration. Update claims only after reading. A negative answer covers only the scope the institution stated; record it in the relevant Survey note (`result: negative` if it closes that sweep).
- On a redirection, keep what is available on what was said orally and what is stated in the written notice. Record registration with the new recipient on its confirmation. If needed, the user checks the state of the request through a channel already in use.
- **Replies to a community post.** When a check is commissioned (and web work authorised), read the replies relevant to the question and the available nested threads. Use the comment count for navigation. To decide the next step, compare the content of the replies with the Search Plan. Keep material leads with their provenance (Survey, or a Source if a reply quotes or encloses a record), and record the actions needed and their state in the current Search Plan.
- On a reply: update the status line in the letter (in its language) and the row in the letters index in the same edit; file the reply (`.pdf` beside the letter in the letters folder if it is correspondence only, or via `add-archive-record` if it is evidence).
- Take the next follow-up date from the institution's acknowledgement or from what the user agreed; do not set a universal response time. The agent does not monitor the mailbox; follow-ups are drafted when the user asks or reports a reply.
