# State portals and official requests

Read when the question needs a ZAGS (civil-registry) record or an unpublished document held by an institution. The letter's contents, attachments and recording the result follow one procedure, in `correspondence.md`; this file covers choosing the service and the delivery method. First identify the custodian by place, date and type of event (`ex-ussr-locale-guide.md`), then check its official channel. A services portal does not replace searching the archive if the register has been transferred there. Portal lookups only once the user has authorised web work for this task (`privacy-and-safety.md`, section 0); **the agent never submits an application** — it drafts, the user submits.

## Choose a workable route

- From the page of the specific service, find out who is entitled to information about a deceased person, how kinship or representation is proven, what result is issued, how it is received, and the cost and timing. Leave unknown conditions unknown; do not carry them over between countries, services and institutions.
- When documents proving kinship are needed, build the chain only from material in hand. A missing link becomes a separate check or request; it does not stop the other sources.
- Distinguish fully remote submission and receipt, online submission with collection in person, and in-person/postal application. The labels `online`, `hybrid`, `offline` may be used in the `Search Plan -` note where they help the choice. The submission channel by itself does not determine delivery: an electronic application may end in a paper document. A remote service may also have territorial restrictions.
- Choose the route by expected value, accessibility, cost and the applicant's rights. There is no need to exhaust every online service before a useful written request. For an applicant abroad, check the specific options for delivery, consulates or a representative and the power-of-attorney requirements; do not promise their availability without checking.
- Prepare the known form data, the text and the attachments the user agrees to in a letter note in the letters folder (`correspondence.md`, section 4). Login, personal signature, submission and payment are done by the user. Never ask for passwords, codes or tokens. Leave the step waiting on the user in the `Search Plan -` note and continue the research.

This is navigation, not a ruling on access rights. Before the user applies, read the current service page and record the URL, date and the conditions actually checked in the letter note. The existence of a site or an old instruction does not prove the operation is available now.

## What to ask for and how to keep the reply

A certificate (свидетельство), a reference/extract (справка/витяг) and a copy of the act record may contain different sets of fields. If the research needs the parents, the informant/applicant, the original place or a later annotation, name those details and ask which format is available; do not promise that any reference contains every column. For a personnel, pension or military file, check the custodian, survival and accessibility of the specific document; a general retention period does not prove the file was destroyed.

Follow `correspondence.md`, section 4: the letter note in the letters folder and its row in the letters index are the record; do not create a parallel register. Keep the text actually sent (as the user reports it), the attachments, the acknowledgement, the date and the case number. A reply with attachments becomes an artefact — via `add-archive-record` if it is evidence; a reference paraphrasing a record and a copy of the same record do not form independent confirmations. A refusal or negative answer covers only the scope the institution stated, and silence does not prove the registers are lost.

For official use abroad, check the receiving country's, the specific country pair's and the institution's requirements for translation, power of attorney and legalisation. For research reading, do not introduce these procedures automatically.
