# Russia: ZAGS certificates, repression and rehabilitation files, cemetery records

This reference helps choose the kind of ZAGS (civil registry) certificate and work out the procedure for requesting Russian files on repression, documents on disenfranchisement and burial records. Submission channels and the request itself are described in `government-portals.md` and `correspondence.md`; requests are drafted in the letters folder and sent by the user, never by the agent. Before applying, check the current rules (section 5) and the procedure of the specific institution.

## 1. ZAGS certificate and the line «Иные сведения» (Other information)

1. Besides a repeat certificate (повторное свидетельство), a ZAGS office issues certificates (справки) of state registration of an act on the forms of [Order of the Ministry of Justice of Russia of 01.10.2018 No. 200](https://base.garant.ru/72066628/). Certificates of birth, marriage and divorce, adoption, establishment of paternity, change of name and death (forms No. 2, Nos. 4–10, No. 12) have the line «Иные сведения». Under [para. 7 of the Rules for completion](https://base.garant.ru/72066628/f7ee959fd36b5699076b35abf4f52c5c/), on a written application, it receives information from the act record that is not in the main lines of the form: the name of the record's line, then text identical to the record. Information on adoption is not entered in a birth certificate, nor the applicant's place of residence if the registration was declared by a natural person.
2. In the application, list the record lines needed by name: for example, information on the parents (place of birth, citizenship, nationality, place of residence), the last place of residence of the deceased, the grounds for restoring the record, the details of the certificate issued, information on corrections and changes made. A general request for "all information" does not specify which lines to enter. The set of columns depends on the record form in force on the date of registration: do not promise that the requested line exists or is filled in.
3. Certificates are generated in the information system of the Единый государственный реестр записей актов гражданского состояния (Unified State Register of Civil Status Records; Rules, para. 2). If the record is not in the register, the paper record is checked ([143-FZ, art. 9 para. 4](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_16758/1b062c16d27cfdeecb6e2dd2fcc76e226df3c11f/)). Act books of first copies are kept at the ZAGS office for a hundred years and then transferred to the state archive ([art. 77](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_16758/7bffa90c9f7761365b38aa5eb603a386d4c6c4c5/)). Books of second copies of records made before 01.10.2018 are kept by the regional body in charge of ZAGS; they are destroyed after the first copies are transferred to the archive, and if the book of first copies has not survived, the book of second copies is not destroyed ([219-FZ, art. 3](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_200018/30b3f8c55f65557c253227a65b908cc075ce114a/)). If the certificate has an omission, an illegible place or a discrepancy, one can ask for it to be checked against the paper record and where the book is kept. Do not assert in advance that the paper record contains more information.
4. A repeat certificate and a certificate about a deceased person are issued to a relative or other interested person; to anyone else — on a notarised power of attorney from a person entitled. On receipt, an identity document and documents confirming the entitlement are presented; their list is set by the Ministry of Justice of Russia ([143-FZ, art. 9 paras. 2, 7](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_16758/1b062c16d27cfdeecb6e2dd2fcc76e226df3c11f/)). Check the requirements of the specific service by `government-portals.md`. To prove kinship, build from the documents available a chain of records from the applicant to the person in the request; for a collateral line it passes through common ancestors. A missing link is a separate request, not a reason to stop the rest of the search.
5. The request is made in person, by post or via Госуслуги (Gosuslugi); for a postal or electronic request the document is issued by the ZAGS office named in the request. Submission and receipt are also possible through an МФЦ (multifunctional centre) (art. 9 paras. 4–5).
6. A change-of-name record documents the link between former and new surname, first name and patronymic. Information on corrections and changes made (including after adoption, establishment of paternity, change of name — 219-FZ, art. 3 part 7) may point to a related record and another ZAGS office: if relevant to the question, include it in the list of lines.

## 2. Rehabilitation and access to political repression files

1. Release, amnesty, removal from the special-settlement register and rehabilitation are different decisions. A document on one of them does not prove another; record the document's title, the issuing body and the date.
2. [Law of the RF of 18.10.1991 No. 1761-1](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/) makes eligible for rehabilitation, in particular, those convicted on political grounds, those subjected to criminal repression by decisions of extrajudicial bodies (ВЧК — ГПУ — ОГПУ, НКВД, МГБ, «особых совещаний», «двоек», «троек» and others), and those subjected administratively to exile, banishment, assignment to special settlement, or forced labour under restriction of freedom, including in «рабочих колоннах НКВД» (NKVD labour columns) ([art. 3](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/f130ce721f7ec7ae964cee1f78e85b0bd0f599ec/)).
3. An application for rehabilitation may be filed by the repressed person, any person or a public organisation: for administrative repression — to the internal affairs bodies, for the rest — to the prosecutor's office, at the location of the body that took the decision or at the applicant's place of residence; the term of consideration is no more than three months ([art. 6](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/9876ee8551edda745ca366c4d598ab04e1ae3986/)). The internal affairs bodies issue a certificate of rehabilitation or report a refusal ([art. 7](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/4d960c434f83573ee534fb2ee81e42f6f6602365/)); the prosecutor's office reviews cases decided by courts and extrajudicial bodies and issues certificates or sends the case to court ([art. 8](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/810c500e1be7b8f1695fc62fc72c6287de4ad0c0/), [art. 9](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/3d68f8c3332b4824add0ba142283f12e235ddc73/)). Rehabilitation documents issued in the former Union republics are valid in Russia ([art. 11](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/8828f3a9d2367f5536a49febbc2bd2eadc15054d/)).
4. Children who were with their repressed parents in places of detention, exile, banishment or special settlement, and children left as minors without the care of groundlessly repressed parents, are recognised as repressed and eligible for rehabilitation ([art. 1.1](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/ea5400062fdc57c6c3d34dfacc9ff373ff568568/)). Children, spouses and parents of those shot or who died in detention and were posthumously rehabilitated are recognised as victims ([art. 2.1](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/087945428823ec4c80ed37dc94a6e275bb456ae7/)). Family members may have their own certificates: search for them separately from the head of family's file and compare the places and dates they give.
5. The rehabilitated person, and with their consent or after their death their relatives, may examine the materials of terminated criminal and administrative cases and receive copies of documents; other persons — under the procedure established for state archives. The rehabilitated and their heirs may receive manuscripts, photographs and personal documents surviving in the file. On application, the archive, if it holds the information, must report the time, cause of death and place of burial of the rehabilitated person ([art. 11](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1619/8828f3a9d2367f5536a49febbc2bd2eadc15054d/)).
6. [Regulation No. 375/584/352](https://gosarhiv-ra.ru/images/Docs/pravovoiDoc/Fiderachiy/N_375_584_352.pdf) covers terminated criminal and administrative cases against the rehabilitated, parts of group cases concerning the rehabilitated, and filtration-check files (paras. 2–3). A relative of a rehabilitated person files an application, presents documents of identity and kinship, and the written consent of the rehabilitated person or a document of their death. A relative of a person on whom a filtration-check file was kept needs, besides the application and proof of identity and kinship, a power of attorney from that person or their heirs. Heirs at law apply with an application and documents of identity and kinship. For other applicants the procedure depends on their status and documents under para. 6. Para. 15 provides free copying on request of the rehabilitated, persons on whom filtration-check files were kept, their relatives and heirs. The archive may restrict the issue of individual folios or provide copies with information on third parties depersonalised (paras. 9, 14). Searching for filtration files of repatriates — `germany-forced-labour.md`.
7. The Regulation does not govern access to files where rehabilitation was refused or to files not yet reviewed. On a negative conclusion on rehabilitation, a certificate of the results of the review is issued under para. 5. Under [art. 25 part 3 of Law No. 125-FZ](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_1406/2476677c14d65b4e41f542f0e8548dc8226e5084/) the restriction period for personal and family secrets, private life and information threatening a person's safety is 75 years from the creation of the document. It may be lifted earlier by written permission of the citizen or, after their death, of the heirs. In [Determination of the Supreme Court of the RF of 05.07.2019 No. 5-КА19-20](https://vsrf.ru/lk/practice/stor_pdf/1788816), concerning a specific ordinary criminal case, a refusal was held unlawful in view of the applicant's kinship, the expiry of the period and the absence of information on secrecy. On refusal, ask for its grounds and scope, and whether the accessible folios, an extract or a certificate can be obtained. For state and municipal archives there is a separate procedure for restricting access to unclassified documents containing official information of limited distribution ([Rosarkhiv Order No. 38 as amended by Order No. 77](https://archives.gov.ru/documents/prik77_2025.shtml)). The expiry of 75 years does not by itself determine access to the whole file.
8. For information classified as a state secret, [art. 13 of Law No. 5485-1](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_2481/f436d4941451fecfec8e1bcf4a5beb0eb74b5e36/) sets a classification period of up to 30 years, extendable in exceptional cases on the conclusion of an interdepartmental commission. Under [art. 15](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_2481/d316e5de93ba43e5ecf9308aacd504443fdf654c/) an official request for declassification may be filed, including with a state archive. Three months is the term for consideration and a reasoned reply if the recipient is competent to decide the matter. Otherwise the recipient forwards the request to the competent body or commission within a month and notifies the applicant.
9. In a ZAGS death certificate for a person whose fate after arrest was not established, the date and cause may reflect the official version. [Note of the KGB under the Council of Ministers of the USSR to the CC CPSU of 25.12.1962 No. 3265-С](https://bukovsky-archive.com/2016/07/01/25-december-1962-3265-s/) reports the practice under an instruction of 1955. Families of those shot by extrajudicial decisions were told of ten years in the camps and death in detention. Where necessary the death was registered with ZAGS with a date within ten years of the arrest and a fictitious cause. For court sentences the note describes a different procedure. Keep the date and cause as information of the certificate and check them against the case materials. For a rehabilitated person the information under art. 11 of Law No. 1761-1 may be requested from the custodian, if the archive holds it.
10. Look for the file with the successor of the body that conducted the investigation or took the decision, or in the state archive to which it was transferred: work from the place of arrest, investigation and decision, not only from the place of birth. Administrative files of special settlers and exiles may be held in the information centres of the MVD at the place of settlement or transferred to state archives (example — `chechnya-deportation.md`, section 6). Establish the custodian of the specific file from the institution's reply.
11. **Camp, special settlement and criminal case.** Check custodians separately by kind of file. If the place of the camp is established, ask the territorial body of the ФСИН (Federal Penitentiary Service) whether it holds the relevant body of records or where it was transferred. The [Agreement between Rosarkhiv and FSIN](https://archives.gov.ru/documents/agreement_fsin.shtml) describes the transfer of archival documents for permanent storage; it does not establish the location of a specific file. The [Reference and Information Centre of the Federal Archives](https://sic.rgantd.ru/kuda-obrashatsya/podtverzhdenie-primeneniya-repressii/) recommends the ГИАЦ МВД (Main Information and Analytical Centre of the MVD) as one route for certificates of repression. Limit a reply of "no information" to the registers, names and period named in the reply. If there is no list of registers, note that the scope of the search is unknown. On redirection, record separately the oral communication, the notice of forwarding and the registration with the new addressee per `correspondence.md`.

## 3. Dekulakisation, banishment, disenfranchisement

1. Decisions on dekulakisation and eviction were made at several levels. Under the [instruction of the Central Executive Committee of the USSR of 04.02.1930 «О конфискации имущества, выселении и расселении кулаков»](https://docs.historyrussia.org/ru/nodes/62500) ("On the confiscation of property, eviction and resettlement of kulaks"), lists of households to be evicted were set by raion executive committees on the basis of decisions of kolkhoz members' meetings and meetings of farm labourers and poor peasants, and approved by okrug executive committees; confiscation was carried out by plenipotentiaries of the raion executive committees with the mandatory participation of the village councils, and delivery to remote areas by the OGPU. Look for meeting protocols, lists, property inventories and correspondence in the fonds of village councils, raion and okrug executive committees in state and municipal archives, and for the family's registration file separately (section 2).
2. Banishment and assignment to special settlement by administrative procedure did not require a criminal case. The absence of an archival investigation file does not rule out repression: look for the administrative (personal, registration) file and the executive committees' documents. A certificate of rehabilitation for such measures is issued by the internal affairs bodies (section 2, para. 3).
3. If a list, protocol or certificate states the grounds for dekulakisation or disenfranchisement, copy them verbatim as the source's record and do not turn them into a conclusion about the family's wealth or conduct (`privacy-and-safety.md`, section 5).
4. Those deprived of voting rights (лишенцы) in the 1920s–1930s. Look for lists, registers, protocols of electoral commissions and materials on restoration of rights in the fonds of village councils, executive committees and electoral commissions. An example of the composition — [ЛОГАВ, ф. Р-1907, оп. 2 «Документы о лишении и восстановлении в избирательных правах граждан района», 1927–1937](https://archiveslo.ru/archives/logav/inventory/164337) (LOGAV — Leningrad Oblast State Archive in Vyborg; "Documents on the deprivation and restoration of voting rights of citizens of the raion"): registers and lists of the disenfranchised by village council, extracts from protocols, protocols of the commission for revising the lists and of village electoral commissions. Information on the disenfranchised and the dekulakised may be collected together: for example, the resource «Раскулаченные. Свердловская область» (`seed-sources.md`) also includes those deprived of voting rights. Check both groups of documents.
5. Forced labour in «рабочих колоннах НКВД» is expressly named in art. 3 para. "в" of Law No. 1761-1; a certificate of rehabilitation for such measures is issued by the internal affairs bodies, and the decision is taken on the specific person. Look for information on those mobilised into labour columns during the war, including Soviet Germans, at the place where the column worked and in the MVD information centre: for example, [ОГАЧО](https://archive74.ru/nahozhdenie-v-trudarmii) (OGAChO — United State Archive of Chelyabinsk Oblast) reports a card index of those mobilised into the ИТЛ «Бакалстрой — Челябметаллургстрой» (Bakalstroi — Chelyabmetallurgstroi corrective labour camp) and documents in the Information Centre of the ГУ МВД России for Chelyabinsk Oblast.

## 4. Cemeteries and burials

1. The burial register is kept by the cemetery administration. Under the [recommendations МДК 11-01.2002](https://meganorm.ru/Data2/1/4294845/4294845854.htm) a burial is registered on the basis of the death certificate, recording the surname, first name and patronymic of the deceased, the dates, the numbers of the quarter, sector and grave, and the person responsible for the grave. The plot and grave number help find the headstone and check neighbouring entries. The recommendations are not binding: check the composition of a specific register against the register itself. Request — per `correspondence.md`; do not collect the current contacts of a living responsible person.
2. The general procedure for searching for burials and assessing the inscription, the transcription and the cemetery register separately — `source-strategy.md`, section 6, "Burial search and checking candidates". Electronic databases of cemeteries and funeral services may have been compiled later and from different materials: find out what the entry was made from.
3. Published necropolises of the early twentieth century — «Московский некрополь» (Moscow Necropolis, 1907–1908), «Петербургский некрополь» (Petersburg Necropolis, 1912–1913) and vol. 1 of «Русский провинциальный некрополь» (Russian Provincial Necropolis, 1914) — preserve information on those buried as of publication and remain a source even if the grave or monument is lost. Electronic copies and limits of use — `seed-sources.md`, section "Maps, churches and cemeteries". A person's absence from a necropolis does not mean there was no burial.
4. On information about the death and place of burial of the rehabilitated and checking a late ZAGS certificate, see section 2, paras. 5 and 9.
